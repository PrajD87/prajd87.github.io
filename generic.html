About Me

## Senior Data Analyst

I am a results-driven Senior Data Analyst with 8+ years of experience transforming complex data into actionable insights across **IT, telecommunications, digital media, and oil & gas** industries. I specialize in data analysis, business intelligence, statistical modeling, reporting automation, and translating business requirements into data-driven solutions. My expertise includes **SQL, Python, R, Tableau, Power BI, Excel, ETL, data visualization, statistical analysis, and predictive modeling**. I have worked with large and diverse datasets to identify trends, improve reporting processes, support forecasting, measure business performance, and help stakeholders make informed decisions. Throughout my career, I have focused on creating measurable business impact—improving forecasting accuracy, increasing advertising ROI, reducing manual reporting effort, automating repetitive processes, and delivering insights that support operational and strategic decisions. I enjoy solving complex business problems with data and turning numbers into clear, meaningful stories that drive better decisions.
